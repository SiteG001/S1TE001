Why owners call

A second home four hours away is either an escape or a worry. Rarely both at once.

Storms move through the mountains fast. A slow leak becomes a floor. A cleaning crew says it came. A guest arrives to a cold, empty kitchen. None of it announces itself, and all of it lands on you, usually at the worst possible moment.

White Oak Stewardship exists so that stops being your problem. Scheduled eyes on your property, documented in photo and video, with someone local who can act the moment something looks wrong.

How it begins

Four steps, and then you stop thinking about it

  1. A private consultation

    Thirty to sixty minutes, in person or by phone. Your property, your concerns, how often you are here, and what you would rather never handle again.

  2. Your property profile

    Access instructions, systems, preferred vendors, the corners that need watching. Sensitive details are collected securely and never shared.

  3. Your stewardship plan

    A membership level and a visit schedule built around the home, not a package number picked off a list.

  4. Reports, while you are away

    A video walk-through after each visit, notification the moment something looks wrong, and a call before anything is spent on your behalf.
