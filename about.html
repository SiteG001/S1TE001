About

Trust is the whole product

White Oak Stewardship is one person, deliberately. You will always know who is walking through your home.

Katie Mujcic, founder

I have spent my entire career in customer service, and one thing has always brought me the most satisfaction: making life easier for other people.

A vacation home or second residence should be a place of relaxation, not another source of stress. But managing a property from miles away usually means worrying about weather damage, maintenance, vendors, guest readiness, or something unexpected that turns an enjoyable trip into a frustrating one.

Living in North Georgia, where conditions can change fast, I saw how much it matters for homeowners to have a trusted local professional looking after their investment. Whether that means routine inspections, coordinating vendors, preparing a home before an arrival, or simply being reachable when something happens, my goal is to give homeowners complete peace of mind.

White Oak Stewardship exists so that clients know someone they trust is caring for their home as if it were their own.

What makes this different

A private steward, not a service package

Most companies in this space offer either routine property checks or basic concierge assistance. White Oak Stewardship combines professional home stewardship with personal assistance under a single trusted relationship.

  1. Built around your home

    Not a one-size-fits-all package. Tiered memberships, plus the flexibility to customize through à la carte services whenever something extra comes up.

  2. One point of contact

    The same person who inspects your home coordinates your vendors and answers your call. Nothing gets lost in a handoff, because there are no handoffs.

  3. Boutique on purpose

    Ten to twenty member homes at launch, growing to fifty to seventy-five, and never further without adding trusted people first.

  4. Discretion as standard

    Access codes, alarm details and household preferences are collected securely and never shared. We do not photograph or publicize client properties.

Who this is for

Owners who see the home as an investment worth protecting

Second-home owners, vacation homeowners, short-term rental hosts, seasonal residents, busy professionals, retirees and families who want a reliable local representative caring for the property when they cannot be there.

They often live out of town, travel frequently, or simply have schedules that leave no room for the day-to-day of homeownership. What they value is proactive communication, attention to detail, and one professional who can oversee the home, coordinate vendors, prepare for arrivals and handle the unexpected quickly.

If you have ever driven four hours wondering what you will find when you open the door, this service was designed for you.
