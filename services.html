Services

Everything that keeps a home cared for while you are elsewhere

Six service groups, drawn together under one relationship. Most owners begin with oversight and add the rest as they realize how much of it they had been carrying.

01 · Foundation

Home watch & property stewardship

Scheduled visits, walked the same way every time, so a change is obvious the moment it appears. You receive a video walk-through after each one.

  • Scheduled home inspections and detailed video walk-through reports
  • Identification of leaks, cracks, storm damage, environmental concerns and unusual changes
  • Documentation of property condition through photo and video
  • Monitoring for anything outside normal wear and tear
  • Coordination of follow-up when an issue is discovered

02 · Oversight

Vendor & maintenance coordination

The difference between a vendor who says the work was done and a vendor whose work was checked is the entire reason owners hire a steward.

Coordination

  • Communication with cleaning crews, contractors, landscapers and maintenance providers
  • Scheduling and oversight of home services when you are unavailable
  • A trusted local point of contact for anything property-related

Verification

  • Confirmation that work was completed properly and to your expectations
  • Photo documentation of completed work
  • Follow-up when something falls short

03 · Arrival

Home preparation & restocking

You should not spend the first evening of a trip cleaning, shopping and waiting for the house to warm up.

  • Preparing the home for owner arrivals or guest stays
  • Restocking household essentials and requested supplies
  • Grocery and specialty item coordination
  • Making sure the home is comfortable, welcoming and genuinely ready

04 · Short-term rentals

Vacation rental & Airbnb support

Reviews are won and lost in the two hours before a guest walks in. That is the window we cover.

  1. Between stays

    Post-cleaning quality checks, so the turnover is verified rather than assumed.

  2. Before arrival

    Guest arrival preparation, restocking and supply management.

  3. Throughout

    Vendor coordination to keep the guest experience consistent across the season.

  4. Always

    Property oversight for owners who are not local and cannot drop by.

05 · Lifestyle

Private concierge & lifestyle management

Available by membership level, and shaped around the household rather than a fixed menu.

Personal assistance

Errands, arrangements and requests tailored to how the household actually runs.

Occasions

Holiday decorating coordination, event preparation and support before the guests arrive.

Seasonal support

Preparation ahead of the season, and the reset afterwards.

06 · Seasonal

Seasonal & customized home services

North Georgia weather turns quickly, and a mountain home that is ready for it costs far less than one that was not.

Seasonal transitions

Decorating, storage, and switching the home over as the season changes.

Weather preparation

Preparing the property ahead of changing conditions, and checking it afterwards.

Custom requests

Anything specific to your property, through a membership package or à la carte.
