2. What the service is

White Oak Stewardship provides scheduled visual inspection, documentation, vendor coordination and concierge support for private residences. Specifically, the service:

  • Observes and documents the visible, accessible condition of a property
  • Reports findings to the owner and escalates urgent findings by telephone
  • Coordinates licensed vendors and verifies that agreed work was completed
  • Prepares homes for arrivals, restocks, and performs concierge tasks at the applicable membership level

3. What the service is not

This is stated plainly because the boundary protects you as much as it protects us. White Oak Stewardship does not provide:

  • Repairs, maintenance or trade work. Licensed contractors perform licensed work. We coordinate and verify it; we do not carry it out.
  • A licensed home inspection. Inspections under these terms are visual condition checks, not the statutory inspection required for a sale, and they are not performed by a licensed home inspector.
  • Security or alarm monitoring. Visits are scheduled and documented. They are not a patrol and not a substitute for a monitored alarm system.
  • Continuous or 24/7 monitoring. Nothing is observed between scheduled visits. Owners are encouraged to install low-temperature and water sensors to cover that gap.
  • Emergency response. Round-the-clock emergency coordination is not currently offered. Call emergency services first, always.
  • Property management as defined by Georgia real estate law. We do not lease properties, collect rent, or act as a real estate broker or agent.
  • Roof, attic or elevated access. Roofs are observed from the ground.
